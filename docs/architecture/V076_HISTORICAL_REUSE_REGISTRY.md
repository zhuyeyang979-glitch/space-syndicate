## Disposition rules

- `ADOPT_AS_OWNER` means one narrow domain keeps exactly one named Owner.
- `ADAPT_AS_CONSUMER` imports a contract or pattern without importing authority.
- `REUSE_AS_TEST` imports an acceptance pattern or oracle only.
- `REFERENCE_ONLY` records evidence or lineage that cannot execute here.
- `RETIRED` forbids revival and whole-branch merge.
