# V0.7.2 Three-Wing Domain Registry

This registry binds the frozen V0.7.2 Core, AI, Player, Save, and RNG contracts while all implementations remain detached from V0.6 production.

The Unified Track emits only standard L1 facility definitions. Personal DBG owns twelve genesis-only Starter instances, their permanent definition-derived zero cost, voluntary Starter-plus-standard merge, and exact Save identity. Six-color Assets creates an initialized owner with six zero balances and six zero remainders.

AI receives owned card identity, origin, cost, merge family, level, and `legal_targets`. Legal targets must come from `v072.personal_dbg.legal_target_input.v1`, bound to the current DBG revision and a named target authority; absence or staleness fails closed. Player presentation consumes `starter_badge=true` and resolves `card.badge.starter` through the canonical presentation asset contract.

The registry declares zero production connections, zero V0.6 mutations, and zero dual writes. Human fun remains unproven and human testing remains required.
