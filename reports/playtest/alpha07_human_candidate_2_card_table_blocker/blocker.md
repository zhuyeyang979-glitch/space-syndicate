## Observed blockers

1. An acquired commodity card was not visibly usable in a clearly separate commodity hand.
2. The central public arrangement read as a debug/data summary instead of a table of cards.
3. An accepted ordinary card did not visibly leave the hand as it entered public resolution.
4. The arrangement showed numbers/summary fields rather than real card faces.
5. The arrangement did not behave as a collapsible overlay that restored the map view.
6. AI players did not visibly play cards (or provide an explicit visible PASS).
7. The sushi track appeared to wobble without a human-observable authoritative next-player handoff.
8. Card zones and transitions were not intuitive enough to complete a legal drag-and-play action.

The user also reported long new-game startup; the existing loading/progress repair remains in scope for performance follow-up, but it does not erase the eight card-table blockers above.

No STEP13–15 claim was made. Existing Candidate 2 evidence remains untouched.
